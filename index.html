Our Approach

Identify. Assess. Control. Train. Implement. Measure. Improve.

Three beliefs behind every engagement.

Safety is a leadership responsibility

Strong safety cultures begin with leadership. Managers and supervisors influence workplace safety through the expectations they establish, the hazards they correct, the behaviors they reinforce, and the accountability they maintain.

Safety cannot be delegated entirely to a safety department. Leaders must understand their role in protecting employees and managing risk.

Safety must be proactive

Organizations should not wait for an accident to discover a hazard. Our approach emphasizes:

Hazard recognitionRisk assessmentWorkplace inspectionsEmployee observationsNear-miss reportingCorrective actionTrainingSupervisor accountabilityIncident preventionContinuous improvement

Employees are part of the solution

Employees performing the work often have valuable knowledge about workplace hazards.

Effective safety programs encourage employees to identify hazards, communicate concerns, participate in safety discussions, and contribute to workplace improvements.
